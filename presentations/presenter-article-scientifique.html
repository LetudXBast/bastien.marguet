Scientific Paper Presentation - Guiding Questions

7 slides - 3 key questions per slide

1. Bibliographic context

  1. What is the paper title, journal, and publication year?
  2. Who are the authors and their institutions?
  3. What is the main topic in one sentence?
Reference

2. Scientific problem

  1. What question does the paper try to answer?
  2. Why is this question important for the field?
  3. What gap in previous studies is identified?
Problem

3. Methodology

  1. What method is used (experimental, numerical, theoretical)?
  2. What are the key variables and parameters?
  3. What are the main assumptions or limitations of this method?
Method

4. Main results

  1. What are the two or three most important results?
  2. Which figure or table best supports these results?
  3. How can you explain one key result in simple words?
Results

5. Discussion

  1. How do the authors interpret their results?
  2. What are the strengths and weak points of the study?
  3. How does this paper compare with previous work?
Analysis

6. Impact and applications

  1. What could be practical applications of this work?
  2. Who can use these results (industry, labs, education)?
  3. What is one potential extension of this research?
Applications

7. Personal synthesis

  1. What did you learn from this paper?
  2. What is the paper's main take-home message?
  3. What question would you ask the authors?
Conclusion
