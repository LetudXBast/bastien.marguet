Exercise 1

Make me care

Objective

Structure a simple idea and capture attention.

You have 60 seconds.

Introduce yourself - but do not list facts. Make me interested.

You must include
  • Who you are
  • What you study or work on
  • Why it matters - why should I care?

Process

2
Feedback - 2 minutes
3
Round 2 - improve
4
Feedback, then Round 3 - final version
Feedback guide
  • Was it clear?
  • Did I understand what you do?
  • Did I understand why it matters?
  • At what moment did I lose attention?
Exercise 1
Debrief

What usually changes everything

Structure beats overload

A clear path matters more than a long list of facts.

The "why" creates attention

Relevance gives the listener a reason to stay with you.

Too much information = lost attention

More content does not mean more understanding.

Debrief
Core Tool

Every explanation needs 3 things

1

What is it?

Name the object clearly.

2

How does it work?

Give the listener a simple mechanism.

3

Why does it matter?

Show the stake, not just the definition.

Link: too many ideas = no understanding.

Core tool - 10 min

Key message
Body and Voice

Minimal, but useful

Slow down

Give your listener time to build the idea.

Use pauses

Silence gives structure to your message.

Look at people

Check whether understanding is actually happening.

This supports clarity - it does not replace it.

Body and voice - 5 to 10 min
Conclusion

Final ideas

Clarity is a choice.

Understanding is constructed.

If people do not understand, the message failed.
