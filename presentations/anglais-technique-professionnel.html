Internship Presentation - Guiding Questions

7 slides - 3 simple questions per slide

1. Context

  1. What real-world situation shows why this field is important? Use a statistic, a real-world example, or a short anecdote.
  2. What system or process is used to address this problem?
  3. What is the main technical challenge engineers must solve?
Introduction

2. Company

  1. What does the company do?
  2. What is its sector or industry?
  3. How is it linked to your internship topic?
Industrial environment

3. Process

  1. What is the main process or system?
  2. How does it work?
  3. Why does it matter?
Technical core

4. Protocol

  1. What experiment or test do you perform?
  2. What are the main steps of the experiment? Try to summarize them in one sentence.
  3. What equipment or system do you use?
Method

5. Experimental Design

  1. What parameter do you change, how, and why? Example: We change X with Y to see how it affects Z.
  2. What parameter do you measure, and how?
  3. Why do you test this parameter?
Variables

6. Results

  1. What is the main result? Example: When X increases, Y decreases.
  2. How do you interpret this graph?
  3. Is this result satisfactory? Why or why not?
Analysis

7. Conclusion

  1. What are the main results of your work?
  2. Do these results answer the initial problem?
  3. What is the main limitation or next step?
Take-home message
